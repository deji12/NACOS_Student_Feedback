FAQ

Common questions

Can't find your answer? Reach us through the contact details below.

Is my feedback anonymous?

Yes, if you tick "Submit anonymously" your name, matric number and email are not sent with your message.

Who reads what I submit?

The NACOS executive team at BIU reviews every submission. Complaints about staff are passed on through the proper channels.

Will I get a reply?

If you leave an email address we may follow up. Anonymous submissions cannot be replied to.

How long does a response take?

We aim to review submissions within a week, though some issues take longer to resolve.

Can I submit more than once?

Yes. Send a new message for each separate issue, suggestion or commendation.

What should I avoid writing?

Please keep messages respectful and factual. Abusive or false submissions may be ignored.
